                                     🏠📊 House Price Analysis Dashboard 🏠📊

An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing house prices, sales performance, inflation, interest rates, and regional trends.
The project includes advanced DAX calculations, KPI visuals, slicers, bar charts, donut charts, and predictive business insights.

🔧 Tools Used:
- Power BI
- Excel
- DAX
- Data Visualization
  
📌 Features:
- Sales Performance Analysis
- House Type & Region Insights
- Interactive Slicers and Filters
- KPI Cards & Dynamic Charts
- Inflation & Interest Rate Analysis
  
📈 Insights:
- Analyze house price trends by region and city
- Compare offer price vs purchase price
- Track YTD sales and average SQM price
- Understand impact of inflation and interest rates
